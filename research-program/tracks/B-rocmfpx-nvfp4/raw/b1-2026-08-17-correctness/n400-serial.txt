Quantization speeds up LLM inference mainly by reducing the amount of data that must be moved and processed.

Large language model inference, especially token-by-token generation, is often **memory-bandwidth bound** rather than compute-bound. For each generated token, the model may need to load a large portion of its weights from memory into the compute units. If the weights are stored in lower precision, fewer bytes need to be transferred.
