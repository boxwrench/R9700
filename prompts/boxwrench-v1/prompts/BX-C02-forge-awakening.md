# BX-C02 — Forge Awakening

Primary test: full-body articulation, mechanical weight, steam, sparks, and layered lighting.

Reference: `../references/forge-awakening.webp`

## I2V positive prompt

```text
Boxwrench awakens inside the towering forge cradle. His shoulder locks release in sequence, the brass-framed hexagonal chest reactor rises from darkness to a steady warm glow, and he takes one deliberate heavy step onto the platform as the rear pistons retract. A short shower of orange sparks falls behind him and steam rolls low across the floor without hiding his silhouette. The camera makes a slow, stable crane downward to keep his full body framed. No camera cut. Audio: heavy lock releases, hydraulic pistons, one armored footfall, crackling sparks, and a deep reactor tone.
```
