Primary test: hand/tool contact, constrained action, rain, sparks, and reflective ground.

Reference: `../references/convergence-wars.webp`

```text
Kneeling on the rain-dark citadel deck, Boxwrench braces the damaged conduit with his left hand and turns the heavy wrench exactly one quarter-turn with his right. The coupling seats with a firm mechanical stop and emits a brief, localized burst of sparks; nothing explodes. Rain beads and runs across chrome and brass while shallow water ripples around his planted knee. The camera performs a small, smooth clockwise arc, keeping both hands and the coupling visible. No camera cut. Audio: steady rain, metal strain, one wrench impact, a brief electrical crackle, and distant machinery.
```
