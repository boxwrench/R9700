Primary test: BX-77 identity, reflective materials, subtle controlled motion, and exposure stability.

Reference: `../references/bx77-anchor.webp`

## I2V positive prompt

```text
Boxwrench remains planted beneath the narrow overhead light, immense and disciplined. His single crimson visor beam sweeps once from left to right, the brass-framed hexagonal chest reactor gives one restrained pulse, and faint steam curls past the layered shoulder armor. Mirror-polished chrome and deep brass reflections remain physically consistent as the camera performs a very slow, steady push in. No camera cut. Audio: a low reactor hum, one quiet scanner sweep, distant citadel machinery, and soft steam.
```

```text
Boxwrench (BX-77), a seven-foot sentient heavy armored robot with a mirror-polished chrome-titanium chassis, deep brass command plating, heavy gold-headed rivets, a brass-framed hexagonal chest reactor, a high armored collar, layered shoulder pauldrons, a raised gold helmet crest, and one dark horizontal visor containing a single crimson scanning light, stands planted beneath a narrow overhead light inside the Aegis Citadel. Cinematic 1990s science-fiction cel-animation language rendered with detailed industrial materials, hard inked edges, restrained black, chrome, brass, and one crimson accent. His visor beam sweeps once from left to right, the chest reactor gives one restrained pulse, and faint steam curls past his shoulders while the camera performs a very slow, steady push in. No camera cut. Audio: a low reactor hum, one quiet scanner sweep, distant citadel machinery, and soft steam.
```

Use the common negative prompt from `STANDARD.md`.

Watch for: helmet or crest mutation, extra red lights, crawling reflections, breathing-like armor deformation, or an unrequested cut.
