# Boxwrench character identity capsule

Authoritative visual characteristics derived directly from the anchor asset:
[`/ai/github/boxwrench/public/images/lore/bx77-anchor.webp`](/ai/github/boxwrench/public/images/lore/bx77-anchor.webp)

---

## 1. Core visual archetype
- **Identity:** Robot Knight / Machine Paladin.
- **Proportions:** Imposing, heavy humanoid frame; broad-shouldered, tall, grounded stance; physical weight and mechanical deliberate movement.

## 2. Head & helmet
- **Structure:** Rounded medieval knight-style close helm / burgonet silhouette with an articulated longitudinal central crest running from front to back.
- **Collar / Neck:** Tall, reinforced raised gorget (bevor-style high metal collar) shielding the lower jaw and neck.
- **Visor / Optics:** Deeply recessed horizontal visor slit with subtle, cold internal sensor illumination (subtle red/amber optic trace deep within dark visor shadow).

## 3. Torso & chest armor
- **Chestplate:** Heavy segmented plate armor with articulated breastplate geometry and prominent brass/gold-trimmed borders.
- **Central Core:** Distinctive central hexagonal mechanical insignia/receptacle centered on the upper chest.
- **Abdomen:** Segmented horizontal rib plates offering articulated torso flexibility, secured with exposed mechanical bolts and fasteners.
- **Backpack / Power Unit:** Compact, angular industrial backpack/generator module mounted to the back armor with visible vents and cable ports.

## 4. Limbs & articulation
- **Shoulders (Pauldrons):** Curved, tiered shoulder armor with brass perimeter rims, rounded cap contours, and mechanical rivet studs.
- **Arms & Gauntlets:** Segmented cylindrical vambraces, articulated elbow couters, and heavy-duty mechanical gauntlets with individual segmented metal finger plates.
- **Legs & Sabatons:** Heavy steel greaves protecting the shins, reinforced brass knee poleyns, and thick industrial sabaton boots with grooved soles.
- **Faulds / Skirt:** Flexible dark chainmail/mesh draped skirt elements hanging from the reinforced waist utility belt.

## 5. Color palette & materials
- **Primary Metal:** Burnished gunmetal steel and cold brushed chrome with realistic specular highlights, surface micro-scratches, and machined seam lines.
- **Secondary Accent:** Rich brushed brass / warm antique gold trim along armor bevels, joints, rivets, and border rims.
- **Under-layer:** Matte dark charcoal synthetic fabric and fine metallic chainmail mesh.
- **Lighting Response:** Sharp technical reflections catching cyan/white diagnostic beams and warm ambient forge/furnace light.
